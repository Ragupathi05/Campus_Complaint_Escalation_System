===========================================================

   - Submit complaints using a 4-level cascading location picker.
   - Upload issue photos, track real-time visual step-timeline, comment, rate resolution (1-5 stars), reopen within 3 days.

   - Operational lead for specific department (Electrical, IT, Cleaning, Plumbing, Maintenance).
   - Triage incoming department queue, assign/reassign staff, review "After Repair" photos, approve or reject resolution.
3. STAFF (ROLE_STAFF):
   - View assigned tasks sorted by priority, update status (IN_PROGRESS, WAITING_PARTS), upload mandatory "Before & After" photos, mark RESOLVED.
4. ADMIN (ROLE_ADMIN):
   - System Administrator. Manages Users, Departments, Campus Location Hierarchy, Categories, SLA rules.
   - NOTE: Admin NEVER assigns daily complaints to staff (delegated to Dept Head).
5. HIGHER OFFICIALS (ROLE_HIGHER_OFFICIAL):
   - Principal / Vice Principal / Dean. Executive read-only view of severe, SLA-escalated tickets with administrative note capability.
