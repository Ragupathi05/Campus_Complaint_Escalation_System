Workflow

Student submits complaint

↓

Complaint is stored

↓

Complaint automatically reaches corresponding Department Head

↓

Department Head assigns complaint to one staff member

↓

Staff accepts complaint

↓

Status changes to IN_PROGRESS

↓

Staff resolves complaint

↓

Student verifies

↓

Complaint Closed

If complaint is not updated within configured time

↓

Automatically change status to ESCALATED

↓

Notify Department Head and Admin

↓

Department Head reassigns another staff if required
