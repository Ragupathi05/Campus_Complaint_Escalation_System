Project Description
The Campus Complaint Escalation System allows students to register complaints related to campus issues.
Examples:
- Electrical
- Plumbing
- Internet
- Hostel
- Cleaning
- Library
- Laboratory
- Security
Workflow:
Student logs in
↓
Creates Complaint
↓
Complaint automatically reaches the respective Department Head
↓
Department Head assigns it to a staff member
↓
Staff accepts the complaint
↓
Staff updates progress
↓
Staff resolves complaint
↓
Student verifies
↓
Complaint closes
If the complaint is not updated within the configured number of days:
↓
System automatically changes status to ESCALATED
↓
Admin and Department Head are notified
↓
Department Head may reassign another staff member
